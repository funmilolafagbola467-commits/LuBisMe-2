Sending crypto for the first time

1. Get a wallet app. Binance, Trust Wallet, and Blockchain.com are common, free choices — download one and create an account.

2. Fund your wallet. Most apps let you buy crypto directly with a card or bank transfer inside the app.

3. Copy the address above for whichever coin you want to send — or scan the QR code directly from your wallet app's "Send" screen.

4. Paste the address into your wallet app's "Send" or "Withdraw" screen, enter the amount, and confirm. Double-check the network matches (e.g. TRC-20 vs ERC-20 for USDT) — sending on the wrong network can lose the gift.

5. That's it. The gift goes directly to the creator — it usually arrives within a few minutes, depending on the network.
