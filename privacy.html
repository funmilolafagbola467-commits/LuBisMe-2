Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run your page: your email, name, wallet addresses, subscription details, and any notes that senders leave.
  • Your name, bio and wallet addresses are public on your gift page, by design. Anything on a blockchain is public too.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we will never ask for your private keys or seed phrase.

1. Who we are

LuBisMe ("LuBisMe", "we", "us") runs lubisme.com, a website where people keep their crypto wallet addresses on one page so friends, fans and family can send them gifts. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies together with our Terms of Service.

We handle personal information in line with applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your display name, and your password. Your password is stored in scrambled form by our login provider, and we cannot see it.
  • Page details: your bio, your picture if you add one, your page link name, and your plan.
  • Wallet addresses that you add.
  • Subscription details: your plan, its status, billing dates, the amount, and reference codes from Paystack. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Notes left by senders: the sender's name, message, transaction ID and coin, and the time the note was sent.
  • Technical information: standard details such as IP address, device and browser type, which our hosting and security systems record when you visit the site.

People who send a gift do not need an account. If a sender leaves a note, we collect only what they choose to type.

3. What is public

Your display name, bio, picture, link name and wallet addresses are shown on your gift page to anyone who has your link, and search engines may find the page.

Wallet addresses and the transactions linked to them are also public on their blockchains. Anyone can look up an address and see its activity. Please do not add an address that you do not want connected to your LuBisMe page.

Your email address, your plan and your subscription details are private. They are never shown on your page.

4. How we use your information

  • to create your account, log you in and keep it secure;
  • to show your gift page and let senders copy your wallet addresses;
  • to process your subscription;
  • to send emails: account confirmation, password resets, alerts when a sender leaves you a note, and messages about your account or plan;
  • to prevent fraud, spam and abuse, for example by limiting how many notes one page can receive;
  • to follow the law and to respond to support requests.

We do not sell or rent your email address or any other personal information.

5. Email alerts about notes

When a sender leaves a note on your page, we email you what they typed (their name, message and transaction ID). The sender never sees your email address. The note is not verified, and the email says so. Please check your own wallet to confirm that a gift has arrived.

6. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run LuBisMe. They only receive the information they need to do their job:

  • Supabase for our database and login system.
  • Vercel for hosting the website.
  • Paystack for processing subscription payments.
  • Resend for sending emails.
  • Small tools that load when a page opens: Google Fonts (for fonts), jsDelivr (for a code library), and a QR code image service (api.qrserver.com), which receives the wallet address shown in a QR code so it can draw the picture.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or if LuBisMe is sold or transferred to another owner. We will tell you if that changes who holds your information.

7. Browser storage

We store a small amount of data in your browser to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies or advertising trackers.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile, wallet addresses, the notes left for you and your subscription records from our database. Some things we cannot remove: Paystack and our other providers keep their own records under their own policies, and nobody can delete data from a blockchain. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, or to deal with fraud or disputes.

9. Keeping your information safe

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access rules that limit each account to its own private data. No website is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you do not use elsewhere. LuBisMe will never ask for your private keys, seed phrase or recovery phrase. Never share them with anyone.

10. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or stop using it for a particular purpose. You can edit your wallet addresses at any time from your dashboard. To make any other request, email support@lubisme.com. We may need to confirm that you are the account owner first.

If you are in Nigeria and are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Depending on where you live, other rights may apply.

11. Children

LuBisMe is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

12. Information stored in other countries

Our service providers may store or process information in countries outside Nigeria. By using LuBisMe you understand that your information may be transferred there.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will post it here and, where we can, tell you by email.

14. Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email support@lubisme.com.

LuBisMe is an intermediary website only. We never hold or process gift funds.
